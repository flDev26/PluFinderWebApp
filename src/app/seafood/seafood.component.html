Preparation
  • Ensure that the steamer is on and has run one cycle for the day.
  • The steamer self replenishes its own water, so there is no need to pour any in.
Seasoning the Shrimp
  • Place the fresh shrimp directly on the steamer tray. Do not place any other items on the tray; ONLY SHRIMP may be steamed.
  • Season the srimp with an even coat. The customer can choose to go light, medium, or heavy on the seasoning.

Steaming the Shrimp
  • Set the steamer to a temperature of 212°F (100°C).
  • Place the steamer tray with the seasoned shrimp into the steamer.
  • Steam the shrimp for approximately 5-7 minutes, or until they turn pink and opaque.
Temperature Check
  • Use a food thermometer to check the internal temperature of the shrimp.
  • The shrimp should reach an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C) to ensure they are fully cooked.
Serving
  • Use provided gloves. Everything inside the steamer is hot and will cause burns.
  • Carefully remove the shrimp from the steamer tray.
  • Transfer them to a seafood bag and wrap them.
Cleanup
  • After the steamer has cooled, disassemble and clean all parts. Turn the steamer off when the day is over.
