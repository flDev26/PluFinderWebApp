About This App

The "PLU Finder" is a static web app that primarily helps users associate product names to corresponding PLUs(Product Lookup Codes). Furthermore, this web app seeks to help new employees or cross functioning employees by providing them with tips and information relevant to their working area. This app only tracks products sold by weight or by quantity, so products that possess a UPC(Universal Product Code) are omitted.

Currently, the purpose of this web app is to provide a secondary method of PLU visualization. For some departments, a visual aid of products in question can better assist employees with PLU familiarization.

A quick reference tool. Simple, but effective.

Use Cases

  • Cashier:

    Useful in helping identify produce PLUs while at a checkout register.

  • Meat Market/Deli:

    Applicable when printing price tags for meat products.

  • Deli/Curbside:

    Serves as a way to recommend or select a substitution for products no currently in stock.

  • Bakery

    Provides baking times and temperatures for bread and pastries.
